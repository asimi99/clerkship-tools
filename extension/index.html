Stem Coach for UWorld

A Chrome extension that opens Stem Coach in a panel beside the UWorld question you are on. Press Coach this question and it reads the stem, the answer choices and any images, then walks you to the answer one decision at a time.

Download the extension (.zip)

Install (one time, about a minute)

  1. Unzip the download. You get a folder called stem-coach-uworld.
  2. In Chrome, go to chrome://extensions.
  3. Turn on Developer mode (top right).
  4. Click Load unpacked and pick the stem-coach-uworld folder.
  5. Pin it from the puzzle-piece menu if you like.

Use

  1. Open a question in UWorld. A Stem Coach tab sits on the right edge.
  2. Click it (or the toolbar icon, or Alt+S) to open the panel. Drag its left edge to resize.
  3. Press Coach this question (or Alt+C). When you move to a new question the button glows.
  4. Highlighted text wins: select part of a page and press Coach to coach just that.

Read only: it never clicks, answers or submits anything in UWorld, and it reads only the question and choices, not the explanation. Sign in inside the panel to use your account. The guest daily limit applies otherwise.
