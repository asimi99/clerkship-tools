Privacy

Asim Ibrahim's Clerkship Tools is a free set of study tools for medical students.

What is stored

If you sign in, your email address and an account ID are stored with Supabase so your progress can follow you between devices. If you sign in with Google, only your name, email and profile picture are shared with this site. Your Google password is never seen.

Stem Coach history and practice exam scores are saved to your account so you can see them later. A daily usage count is kept to stop abuse.

What is sent to an AI model

Screenshots, text and PDFs you add are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to create coaching and explanations. Do not upload patient information or material you are not allowed to share.

What is never done

Nothing is sold, shared for advertising, or used to contact you.

Deleting your data

Email asimibrahim1999@gmail.com and your account and saved history will be deleted.
